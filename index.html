Tivvo Workshop.

A children’s game for Android.

I’m offering Tivvo Workshop with its editable source and game assets. This page shows the game and explains what’s included in the sale.

▶Watch the gameplay

The game has nine mini-games with 162 levels, 30 separate mathematics stages, six locations, a shop, care activities and local save progress. It works offline, without ads or in-app payments.

      Technical details

      Current installation issue

      Google Play Protect has reportedly marked the APK harmful on a Samsung Galaxy S24. I haven’t confirmed the exact warning or its cause yet. The APK’s signature is valid and it requests no permissions, but those checks do not prove safety. This issue is unresolved; do not bypass the warning. There is no APK download on this page.

      Testing and limitations

      The package includes the actual test report: release builds, core-rule tests, emulator gameplay, a save-preserving update, and layout and language checks. This is not a guarantee for every physical device.

        Development and rights

        AI tools helped with the code, artwork and audio. The available art sources, audio generators, development notes, prompts and asset/licence records are included. A supplied music recording without documented commercial permission was removed.

        The sale agreement will need to define which rights I can transfer. Exclusive copyright in AI output and registered trademark rights are not claimed.
